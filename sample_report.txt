# ESG Report Sample

The company is committed to reducing its Scope 1 emissions by 40% by 2030. 
In 2023, we emitted 1500 tonnes of CO2e.
Our energy consumption was 5000 MWh, of which 30% was from renewable energy sources like solar and wind.
The board of directors oversees our climate strategy.
We engage with stakeholders to ensure transparency and accountability.
Our sustainability initiative "Green Future" contributes to SDG 13.
Environmental risks include physical risks from extreme weather events.
