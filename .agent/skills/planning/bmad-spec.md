# Analysis Phase: From Idea to Foundation

The Analysis phase helps you think clearly about your product before committing to building it. Skipping analysis entirely means your PRD is built on assumptions instead of insight.

## Core Methodology

### 1. Brainstorming
*   **Purpose**: Facilitated creative session to explore multiple directions.
*   **Action**: Act as a coach, pulling ideas out through structured exercises rather than generating them for the user.
*   **When to use**: When the solution is not yet crystallized or alternative pressure-testing is needed.

### 2. Research (Market, Domain, Technical)
*   **Purpose**: Investigate competitors, subject-matter expertise, and technical feasibility.
*   **Action**: Build सब्जेक्ट-matter expertise and evaluate architecture options to ground the concept in reality.
*   **When to use**: Entering an unfamiliar domain or when technical capabilities need validation.

### 3. Product Brief
*   **Purpose**: Guided discovery to produce a 1-2 page executive summary.
*   **Action**: Act as a collaborative Business Analyst, articulating vision, target audience, and value proposition.
*   **When to use**: When direction is clear and documenting vision is the priority.

### 4. PRFAQ (Working Backwards)
*   **Purpose**: Write the press release and FAQ before code exists.
*   **Action**: Act as a relentless but constructive product coach, challenging every claim to force customer-first clarity.
*   **When to use**: To stress-test a concept before committing resources.

## Selection Matrix
| Situation | Recommended tool |
| --------- | ---------------- |
| "I have a vague idea, not sure where to start" | Brainstorming |
| "I need to understand the market before deciding" | Research |
| "I know what I want to build, just need to document it" | Product Brief |
| "I want to make sure this idea is actually worth building" | PRFAQ |
| "I want to explore, then validate, then document" | Brainstorming → Research → PRFAQ or Brief |
